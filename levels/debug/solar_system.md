# DEBUG: FULL SOLAR SYSTEM

A rendering / ephemeris stress showcase — **not a mission**.

Every catalogued body (Sun, eight planets, Pluto, and nine major moons) is placed
at its real heliocentric longitude for **2026-07-25**. Moons are decorative
(scenery), so active bodies stay under the loader cap.

The ship parks at Earth: **zoom all the way out** to tour the system and eyeball
many-body render cost. Debug builds only.
